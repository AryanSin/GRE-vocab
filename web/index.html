About Vocab Galaxy & Semantic Clustering

Vocab Galaxy transforms 1,020 GRE vocabulary words from GregMat's Vocab Mountain into an interactive semantic universe.

How Clustering Works:

  1. Dense Embeddings: Each word's definition, grammatical part of speech, and comprehensive synonym list are vectorized into 384-dimensional dense semantic vectors using the state-of-the-art BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 language model.
  2. Strict Semantic Brackets & Outlier Detection: Using hierarchical agglomerative clustering with high pairwise similarity thresholds, words are grouped into 264 tightly cohesive semantic brackets (where only truly similar words are grouped) plus a dedicated Miscellaneous & Outliers bracket for solitary terms.
  3. 2D Topology Mapping: High-dimensional embeddings are projected onto 2D space using t-SNE with cosine metric, preserving local synonym neighborhoods and macro-clusters.
  4. Synaptic Neighbors: Pairwise cosine similarities identify the top 6 closest semantic siblings for each term.

Controls:

  • Hover any word node or pill to instantly inspect its description HTML.
  • Click to lock the word in the right inspector drawer.
  • Scroll / Pinch to zoom; Drag canvas to explore the galaxy.
  • Press / or Ctrl+K anytime to jump to any word.
